About Me

A little bit about myself.

I am a Statistics graduate from Universitas Negeri Jakarta with a strong interest in data, technology, and problem solving.

I currently work in a Data Migration team, working on the migration from SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA. My work involves data extraction, profiling, transformation, validation, and preparation of SAP master data, particularly in the PP and QM areas.

Outside of SAP and data migration, I enjoy building dashboards, automating repetitive processes, and finding better ways to turn raw data into something useful and understandable. I mainly work with Power BI, Excel, DAX, and Power Query, while also exploring SQL, Python, and Power Automate.

I also like experimenting with things outside of work. Sometimes that means learning something new, sometimes building a small project, and sometimes making a completely unnecessary game just because it sounds fun.
